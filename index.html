Extraordinary Ability

Understanding the EB-1A Criteria

Learn about the ten evidentiary criteria used in evaluating extraordinary ability petitions. Explore what each criterion means and the types of evidence that may be relevant.

01

Awards and Prizes

Recognition through nationally or internationally recognized prizes or awards for excellence.

02

Professional Memberships

Membership in associations that require outstanding achievements of their members.

03

Published Material

Published material about the individual and their work in professional or major media.

04

Judging the Work of Others

Participation as a judge of the work of others in the same or an allied field.

05

Original Contributions

Evidence of original contributions of major significance in the field.

06

Scholarly Articles

Authorship of scholarly articles in professional publications or other major media.

07

Artistic Exhibitions

Display of work at artistic exhibitions or showcases.

08

Leading or Critical Role

Performance in a leading or critical role for distinguished organizations.

09

High Salary or Remuneration

Evidence of high salary or significantly high remuneration compared with others in the field.

10

Commercial Success

Commercial success in the performing arts, supported by relevant evidence.

The EB-1A visa is an employment-based immigration category designed for individuals who have demonstrated extraordinary ability in fields such as science, education, business, athletics, and the arts. Applicants must demonstrate sustained national or international recognition and provide evidence that their achievements place them among the small percentage at the top of their field. Building a strong professional profile through recognized achievements, publications, citations, judging activities, and significant contributions can help establish relevant evidence for an EB-1A petition.

National Interest Waiver

Understanding the EB-2 NIW Framework

The National Interest Waiver framework is commonly evaluated through three prongs. Explore the central questions behind each one.

PRONG 01

Substantial Merit and National Importance

Understanding the proposed endeavor and its potential value and importance to the United States.

PRONG 02

Well Positioned to Advance the Endeavor

Examining the individual's education, skills, experience, record of success, and progress.

PRONG 03

Benefit of Waiving the Job Offer

Considering whether, on balance, it would benefit the United States to waive the job offer and labor certification requirements.

The EB-2 National Interest Waiver (NIW) is an employment-based immigration pathway for individuals with an advanced degree or exceptional ability whose proposed work is considered to have substantial merit and national importance. Unlike the traditional employer-sponsored route, eligible applicants may request a waiver of the job offer and labor certification requirements by satisfying the three-prong NIW framework. A strong professional profile can help demonstrate an individual's expertise, track record, and ability to advance their proposed endeavor in the national interest of the United States.
